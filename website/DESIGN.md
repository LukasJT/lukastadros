# Design

Editorial portfolio: cool near-white surface, navy text, cobalt links; Playfair Display headings and DM Sans body. Shared horizontal navigation, generous margins, ruled sections and open content rows. Responsive single-column layouts below 760px. Home uses a split text/portrait composition; internal pages use large headings; travel pairs map with album aside. Portrait and empty collections explicitly await user content. No stock travel photos or invented trips.

## Editor
The owner editor extends the navy/cobalt palette with labeled form fields and native expandable sections. A sticky save strip exposes dirty/saving/saved states. Two-column fields collapse on mobile; photos show alongside their descriptions. The owner-only Edit site link sits at the lower right.
